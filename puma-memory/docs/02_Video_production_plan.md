## 3. Teaser template (all 7) — built at 3840 × 708

**Layout: center hero + side wings**, following the main screen's die-cut (two V-notches at the top, 45° bands → three panels).
- **Center panel** (≈ 1400 px wide at the top, ≈ 2800 px at the bottom): a 16:9 hero window of **≈ 1259 × 708 px** carries the slides, all text and the speaker card.
- **Wings:** KV texture and brand motion, plus extensions of the hero image.
- **The KV's green light beam is the main animation element** (S07): it does the transitions (sweep, flash, follow-a-gesture), reveals the text and moves in the background where it fits the scene. It's animated in After Effects from the KV layer, never generated by AI. The Camera Transitions pack is only support.
- **Upgrade for key moments:** extend selected images to the full 3840 px width with AI outpainting.

## 5. Sphere videos — 3840 × 1920 equirectangular masters

**Rules:** important content within **±30° of the equator** (rows ≈ 640–1280 of 1920); left and right edges match frame-for-frame (invisible seam); repeat or rotate so every side of the room sees it. Convert to the controller's map **with the supplier's template, no auto-stretch**. Test a short clip on the real sphere before the full render.

### S1 · Running puma
- A puma running around the equator as a **seamless loop** (one lap ≈ 10–15 s). Two pumas 180° apart so each side always sees one.
- Size: ≈ 50–60° of arc ≈ **550–640 px** long on the 3840 master.
- Style options: **2D brand silhouette** from the Puma Energy logo (cleanest on LED) · **AI-generated puma** cut into a loop · 3D puma (most cost). Recommendation: silhouette or AI, decided after a look test.

### S2 · Map of the 160 stations
- Paraguay at true globe scale is only ~90 px wide on a 3840 master, too small for 160 points. **Enlarge it to ≈ 90–120° of arc (≈ 960–1280 px)** on the front face, repeated 2–3× around or slowly rotating.
- Stations light up progressively (dots ≥ 10–12 px at 3840), joined by network lines, counter rising to "160 ESTACIONES". Optional globe opener.
- The same map build is reused in Daniel's teaser (T1).
- Needs the 160 stations with coordinates or city/address.
