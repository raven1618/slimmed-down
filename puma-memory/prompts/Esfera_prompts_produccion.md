# Sphere opening · production prompts, in script order

> **SUPERSEDED (Nov 20–24):** these are the first-pass 21:9 sphere prompts. Current rules: every frame that fills the ball is a circular type-A fisheye frame (doc 17 §2–3); P8 (black boot, white flash) is replaced by the client neon closing (doc 19, C3). Use doc 17 + doc 19, not this file.

## How the shape is used
- **Every panorama has its horizon on the equator.**
  - The sky goes up to the top pole, which carries the sun and the flare.
  - The ground goes down to the bottom pole, which carries the light path and the puma.
  - The ball spins in post, so each place becomes a moving world turning behind the puma.
- **Two heroes per panorama, at 28% and 72% of the width.** One faces the front audience and one the back.
  - The left and right edges are open scenery; I blend them together there so the join can't be seen.
- **Locked camera in every Seedance clip.** I add the spin, the whip-spin transitions, the light and the text in post.
- **Settings:**
  - Panoramas: FLUX 3 Image · **21:9 · 4K**.
  - Seedance 2.5: start frame (end frame only where noted) · 5 s · **480p** for previews · audio off.
